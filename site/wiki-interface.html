StartThe interface

What every part of the screen is for.

Five tabs

TabWhat it is for
VISUALSThe clip grid: one row per layer, the output preview, color slots, scenes and the panels of the selected layer.
SHOWThe running order with cues, GO and the timeline.
GALLERYAll visuals, effects and your media with a live preview.
OUTPUTScreens, canvas, window output, test patterns and projection mapping.
CONNECTArt-Net, sACN, OSC, LJ-Link and live DMX values.

Top bar

  • MAPPING switches the mapping mode for keyboard, MIDI, DMX and OSC, and modulation.
  • AUDIO chooses the audio input for equalizers and audio-reactive visuals.
  • BPM: click and type a tempo; next to it the tempo source (Auto, LJ-Link, Ableton Link, OSC, manual) and TAP.
  • Status pills for DMX, OSC, LJ-LINK, LINK and MIDI, the frame rate, the lock for the show, the settings and BLACKOUT.

Fader bar

Always at the bottom: master and blackout, one fader per layer, the effect master, strobe with speed and intensity plus Strobe, Flash, White, W-Flash, Blinder and Beat, tap, the transition time and the scene buttons with Next and Auto.

Settings

The gear opens audio input and output, timecode, MIDI devices, DMX and network, interface size, Update and Report a problem.

Lock mode

The lock at the top: during the show you can still operate everything (faders, clips, scenes, effect strengths, GO, blackout), but nothing can be deleted, added, renamed or loaded. Hold the lock for one second to unlock.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
