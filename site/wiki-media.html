VisualsLogos, videos and text

Your own media as clips.

Logos and images

PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF and SVG up to 25 MB: drag them onto the window or use + Load file in the library. A logo on a white or black background is recognised and the background is removed (Graphic → Remove background, with tolerance).

  • Position, size and rotation, and an animation in time: pulse, spin, bounce, float, wobble or zoom, ½ to 8 beats per round.
  • With the drive Static a graphic stands still.

Videos

MP4, MOV, M4V and WebM. VJ-Link copies them into its library and loops them seamlessly. As a clip a video can loop a part, lock the loop to 4–32 beats so it starts on the beat, fill or fit the screen and play its sound.

✓Tip

MP4 with H.264 and WebM play everywhere. If a file does not play, convert it to one of these.

Text clips

+ Text designs a full-screen text frame: font, size, bold, italic, capitals, alignment, line and letter spacing, color, glow, outline and box. It becomes a clip like a graphic; the pencil on the clip edits it again.

From LJ-Link

Library → LJ-Link (DJ): Now Playing is a lower third with cover, title and artist of the master deck, shown 8, 15 or 30 s after a track change or always; DJ waveform is the scrolling waveform like on the player. In LJ-Link's privacy mode Now Playing stays empty.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
