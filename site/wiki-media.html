VisualsLogos, videos and text

Your own media as clips.

Logos and images

PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF (first frame only) and SVG up to 25 MB: drag them onto a row of the clip grid (they land on that layer; anywhere else: the selected layer) or use + Load file in the library. A logo on a white or black background is recognised and the background is removed (Graphic → Remove background, with tolerance).

  • Position, size and rotation, and an animation in time: pulse, spin, bounce, float, wobble or zoom, ½ to 8 beats per round.
  • With the drive Static a graphic stands still.

Videos

MP4, MOV, M4V and WebM. VJ-Link copies them into its library and loops them seamlessly. As a clip a video can loop a part, lock the loop to 4–32 beats so it starts on the beat, fill or fit the screen and play its sound.

MOV, ProRes and transparent videos

New in 0.1.18: VJ-Link takes MOV files of every kind. What the program plays itself (H.264, HEVC on the Mac, WebM) is copied. Everything else – especially ProRes 422/4444, Animation (QTRLE) and PNG-MOV – is converted on import, with a progress note at the top: with transparency into WebM with alpha, without into MP4 (H.264). Transparency stays: a logo as ProRes 4444 lies over the visuals, in a layer as in the SHOW. A transparent video in the SHOW is set to over the visuals by itself.

✓Tip

For logos, export with an alpha channel: ProRes 4444, Animation, HEVC with alpha or WebM with alpha. Converting takes about as long as the clip runs for short logos; long ProRes films take longer.

✎Note

Converting uses FFmpeg, which comes with VJ-Link as a separate program (license in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt).

Text clips

+ Text designs a full-screen text frame: font, size, bold, italic, capitals, alignment, line and letter spacing, color, glow, outline and box. It becomes a clip like a graphic; the pencil on the clip edits it again.

Clock and time

Library → Clock and time: Clock, Countdown to time (e.g. to 00:00 on New Year's Eve, also past midnight), Countdown (duration) from the moment the clip starts, Stopwatch, Date, BPM display and Timecode display. Every output works out the time from its own clock, so all screens show the same. The type is white; the layer's colors (slot, gradient, rainbow), effects and position act on it as on a graphic.

  1. The section of the time clip, named after the clip on the layer.
  2. Style: clean (bold type), digital (equal-width digits) or LED (light dots like a stage clock; text stays clean).
  3. Seconds on / off (clock and countdowns).
  4. Target time (HH:MM or HH:MM:SS, this computer's clock). With the duration countdown: Minutes and Restart.
  5. What happens after the target: 00:00 or the text at 0 stays for 30 minutes, then it counts to the next time.
  6. Line above, e.g. “DOORS OPEN IN”.
  7. Text at 0: shown instead of 00:00 when the countdown has run out.
  8. Pulse in the last 10 s: the type flashes on every second.
  9. Size: share of the picture width the time takes up.
  • The stopwatch and the duration countdown start when the clip starts on its layer; Restart counts again from now.
  • The timecode display shows the timecode VJ-Link receives (Art-Net, MIDI) or its own timecode clock once it has been started; without timecode it shows --:--:--:--. See timecode.
  • To start a whole cue at a time of day, use the SHOW start Time, see SHOW.

From LJ-Link

Library → LJ-Link (DJ): Now Playing is a lower third with cover, title and artist of the master deck, shown 8, 15 or 30 s after a track change or always; DJ waveform is the scrolling waveform like on the player. In LJ-Link's privacy mode Now Playing stays empty.

!Warning

Showing titles and covers in public: if you put track titles, artists or cover art on screens the audience can see (for example with Now Playing), you may need permission from the rights holders or the venue. LJ-Link's privacy mode hides titles and covers.
