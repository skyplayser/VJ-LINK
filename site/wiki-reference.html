HelpReference

Everything to look up.

OSC

UDP port 8000. Values 0–1, 0–127 or 0–255.

AddressArgumentDoes
/vjlink/ctl/<id>valuesets any control, see the ids below
/vjlink/scene/gonumber from 1recalls a scene
/vjlink/scene/next, /vjlink/scene/prev–next or previous scene
/vjlink/master/dimmer0–1master
/vjlink/blackout0 / 1blackout
/vjlink/bpmBPMsets the tempo (for example 128)
/vjlink/tap–tap tempo
/vjlink/layer/<n>/opacity0–1opacity of layer n
/vjlink/layer/<n>/clipnumberstarts the visual with this number on layer n
/vjlink/slot/<a–e>/rgbr g bsets a color slot

IdControl

tempo.tap, tempo.bpm, tempo.factor, transition.beatstempo and transition
layer.<n>.opacity, .color, .blend, .drive, .clear, .fx.1–.fx.3, .laser.x, .laser.y, .laser.size, .laser.rot, .laser.thick, .laser.xyper layer 1–8
slot.<A–E>.hue, slot.<A–E>.r, .g, .bcolor slots
scene.<1–32>, scene.next, scene.prev, scene.autoscenes
show.go, show.back, show.stop, tc.start, tc.stop, tc.zeroSHOW and timecode
test.toggle, test.next, test.prevtest patterns
bild.<1–5>.hell, bild.<n>.e<k>, bild.<n>.bearbeitenpictures of the projection mapping
clip.<layer>.<clip id>start a clip on a layer

KeyDoes
