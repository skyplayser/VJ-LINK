ControlModulation

Faders that move by themselves.

Modulation makes a fader move by itself. You keep the fader where you want it (the base value), and VJ-Link swings the value around it: in time with the music (LFO) or with the level of the bass, mids or highs (audio). The picture breathes and pumps without anybody touching anything.

Set up a modulation

  1. Open MAPPING → Modulation (LFO, Audio) or press ⇧⌘L. All faders that can be modulated get a dashed turquoise frame.

  2. Click a fader, e.g. layer 2 in the fader bar, or an effect slider on the right. The panel shows its modulation; a new one starts as sine over 4 beats at 50 %.

  3. Choose the source: one of the five LFO shapes, or Bass, Mids, Highs for audio.

  4. For an LFO choose the tempo: ¼ to 32 beats per round.

  5. Set the strength (0–100 %). The meter below shows the signal live.

  6. Click the next fader, or Done / Esc. Modulations are saved with the project.

  1. MAPPING → Modulation (LFO, Audio) switches the mode on (⇧⌘L).
  2. The selected fader (turquoise). Modulated faders show the value in the image in pink.
  3. Source: sine, triangle, saw, square, random, then bass, mids, highs.
  4. Tempo of the LFO: beats per round.
  5. Strength: how far the value swings.
  6. Signal now: the live signal of the source, 0–100 %.
  7. On / Off pauses the modulation, Remove deletes it.
  8. List of all modulations of the project; a click selects one.

LFO: in time with the music

An LFO (low frequency oscillator) is a slow, regular movement. It runs on VJ-Link's beat clock, so it stays in time with LJ-Link, Ableton Link, MIDI clock or tap. Tempo says how many beats one round takes: 1 beat pulses on every kick, 16 beats sweeps slowly over four bars.

ShapeMovementGood for
Sinesoft up and downbreathing opacity, slow color changes
Triangleeven up and down, sharp turnlaser size, rotation back and forth
Sawrises slowly, drops at oncebuild-ups, hue running through all colors
Squarejumps between high and lowon/off in time: layers that blink, hard cuts
Randoma new value every roundeffects that change unpredictably

Strength 100 % swings ±50 % around the base value: a fader at 50 % then moves between 0 and 100 %. At the edges it stops at 0 % or 100 %.

Audio: bass, mids, highs

With Bass, Mids or Highs the fader follows the level of that frequency band. VJ-Link takes it from the audio input (microphone or line-in), or, without an input, from the waveform of the playing track in LJ-Link.

Audio pumps upward from the base value: in silence the value is the base value, on a full hit base value + strength. If there is no room above (e.g. opacity at 100 %), it pumps from below up to the base value instead: silence makes it darker, a hit makes it full.

!Warning

No audio, no movement: without an audio input and without LJ-Link an audio modulation stays still at its base value. Check the level under Settings → Audio or CONNECT → Audio input.

See what the modulation does

The fader itself does not move, so that you can still grab it. Instead it shows what is really in the image:

  • A pink fill with a line shows the value in the image right now.
  • The stronger the swing (level or LFO), the brighter pink the fader, its knob, value and name glow.
  • ∿ after the name marks a modulated fader at any time. Sliders in the clip grid and the effects get a pink bar.

Not modulatable: tempo, transition and every control that picks from a list (color source, blend mode, drive). Each fader has at most one modulation, up to 32 per project.

Modulation and mapping together

A fader can be mapped (MIDI, DMX, OSC) and modulated at the same time. The mapped input moves the base value, the modulation keeps swinging around it. Example: the light desk sets layer 2 to 60 % on its channel, a bass modulation with 40 % pumps it between 60 and 100 %; if the desk pulls it to 0, it pumps between 0 and 40 %.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCause and fix
The fader does not movethat is on purpose: watch the pink fill and the picture
Audio modulation does nothingno audio input chosen or level too low; Settings → Audio, raise the level until the meter moves
Hardly visiblestrength too low, or the base value at the edge (an LFO at 100 % only swings downward); lower the fader or raise the strength
LFO out of timethe tempo is wrong: check the BPM and its source at the top
Too hecticmore beats per round, less strength, or use sine instead of square
