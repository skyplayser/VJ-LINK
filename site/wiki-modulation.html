ControlModulation

Faders that move by themselves.

MAPPING → Modulation (⇧⌘L): click a fader and it swings around its position by itself, the visuals change without anybody touching anything. Modulated faders have a turquoise frame.

SourceMovement
Sine, triangle, saw, square, randoman LFO in time, ¼ to 32 beats per round
Bass, mids, highsfollows the audio input or LJ-Link

Strength sets how far it swings: at full strength ±50 % around the set value. The demo uses modulation for the geometry layer, the bass on the RGB shift and a slow hue cycle of slot E.
