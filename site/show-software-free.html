Short answer

The SHOW tab of VJ-Link is a free show software for events: you build a running order of cues (video, image, camera, black, scene, timecode), start each with GO, or lay scenes and strobe, blinder and blackout on a timeline. Art-Net and MIDI timecode can be received and sent, so lighting desk and picture stay in sync.

What it is used for

  • Corporate events, conferences and award shows: logo, speaker video and intermission loops in the right order.
  • Clubs and festivals: live visuals that the light desk recalls, with a fixed show for the opening and the closing.
  • Theatre, stage and church: cues on GO, a camera cue for the live picture and black between the scenes.
  • Shows with a start at a fixed time of day, and a phone as remote control for the running order.

A show in four steps

  1. Open SHOW and add the first cue: a video, an image, a camera, black or a scene.
  2. Add the next cues and set for each one how it ends: stop, loop or continue by itself.
  3. Press GO to start the next cue, or let the timeline run the scenes and the strobe, blinder and blackout buttons.
  4. Save the show as a package with its videos, so it opens the same way on the show computer.

Questions

Is this show software really free?

Yes. VJ-Link is free and open source (EPL 2.0) for macOS and Windows, without watermark, time limit or account.

Can a light desk start the cues?

Yes, over Art-Net, sACN, MIDI or OSC; GO and the scene change can be mapped to any control.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs locally; only the update check uses the internet.
