Light desksBasics and channel list

Patch VJ-Link like a fixture.

VJ-Link listens to one universe of Art-Net or sACN and behaves like a fixture with 91 channels. Only channels that change take effect, so the mouse, MIDI and the desk can work side by side: a fader that does not move on the desk does not overwrite what you set by hand.

Set up in VJ-Link

  1. Desk and VJ-Link computer in the same network (same IP range). On Windows allow VJ-Link for private networks.

  2. CONNECT → set the Art-Net universe (0 is the first) or the sACN universe (1 is the first) and the start address.

  3. Save the channel list (CSV) and patch VJ-Link on the desk with 91 channels.

  4. Move a fader on the desk: the live values show it, the DMX pill at the top turns green.

Network

  • Art-Net often uses the range 2.x.x.x or 10.x.x.x (subnet 255.0.0.0). Give the VJ-Link computer an address in the same range, for example 2.0.0.50, or switch the desk to unicast to the computer's address.
  • sACN uses multicast. With many universes the switch should support IGMP snooping; for a few universes any switch works.
  • Use a cable, not Wi-Fi, between desk and VJ-Link.

More than the profile

Layers 5–8 and every other control (laser position, slot hue, scenes 1–32, test patterns, timecode …) can be put on any DMX channel with MAPPING → DMX / Art-Net: click the element, move the channel on the desk.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
