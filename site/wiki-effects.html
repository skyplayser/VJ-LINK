VisualsEffects

Up to six per layer, from top to bottom.

Each layer has an effect stack with up to six effects that work from top to bottom. Every effect has a strength; at 0 it does nothing. The first three of each layer lie on the desk channels and on MIDI as Effect 1–3.

All 50 effects

EffectWhat it does
KaleidoscopeMirrors into 3 to 12 segments, rotating slowly.
MirrorLeft onto right, from half strength also top onto bottom.
RGB shiftColor channels drift apart, stronger on the beat.
GlitchLines jump, colors swap, noise.
TubeScanlines, curvature and dark corners.
PixelCoarse pixels, the stronger the coarser.
FeedbackThe last image stays and zooms inwards.
GlowSoft glow around bright areas.
Beat zoomBriefly zooms in on every beat.
InvertInverts the colors.
Color overlayTints the layer in its color source (slot, gradient, rainbow; original = slot A).
ASCIIThe image as a character grid (. : + # @) in the image's colors.
Laser lookTurns any visual into thin, saturated lines on pure black, like a laser. For the projector in haze.
DatamoshBlocks of the last image stay and smear, like a broken video.
Echo trailsColored trails behind everything that moves.
StutterFreezes the image on the beat (eighths). Amount = how long it holds.
Zoom blurMotion blur towards the center, stronger on the beat.
TilesRepeats the image 2× to 5×, every other tile mirrored, changing on the beat.
Neon edgesOnly glowing outlines in the image's colors.
1-bitHard black and white with a pixel grid, like an old monitor.
ShakeThe image jolts and tilts on every beat.
Polar warpBends any visual into a tunnel.
Liquid warpWavy, liquid distortion, pumping on the beat.
Beat slicesHorizontal stripes shift on every beat.
Color wheelHue rotates slowly and jumps on every beat.
PosterizeA few hard color steps, pop art look.
Beat invertFlashes into negative on the beat.
Slit scanThe image enters at the top, below it time slides downwards (time slit).
Zoom feedbackThe image falls endlessly into itself, colors rotating.
VHSColor bleeding, tracking errors and tape noise.
BloomBright areas radiate softly.
VignetteDark edges, tighter on the beat.
WavesThe image ripples, stronger on the beat.
TwirlThe center twists back and forth.
FisheyeBulges the image like a wide-angle lens, pumping on the beat.
LCDRed-green-blue subpixels like a screen seen up close.
HalftonePrint dots like in a newspaper.
DuotoneDarks in slot A, lights in slot B.
FilmGrain, flicker, scratches, warm tone.
EmbossEdges become an embossing.
Pixel sortBright pixels run downwards.
LensColor fringes towards the edge, like a cheap lens.
Luma keyDarks become transparent, layers below shine through.
Color strobeFlashes alternately in slot A and B on the beat.
HoneycombHexagon mosaic.
Spin blurBlurs in circles around the center.
SolarizationBright tones flip over, like in the darkroom.
False colorBrightness becomes a color scale from blue to red.
Beat flipMirrors on the beat, from half strength also top/bottom.
TurbulenceShimmers like air over hot asphalt.

✎Note

Effects that need the last picture (feedback, datamosh, echo, stutter, slit-scan, zoom feedback) keep their own memory per layer.
