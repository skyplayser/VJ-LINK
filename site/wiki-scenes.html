ShowScenes and transitions

The building blocks of a show.

A scene is a snapshot of the layers: which clip runs where, with which colors, effects and opacity. + Save scene stores the current picture; a click on a scene recalls it, and the change between two scenes is the transition: a fade, a wipe, a flash and 12 more.

  1. Transition type for all scenes without their own transition.
  2. Length: quick buttons ✂ (cut), 1, 2, 4, 8, or type any number (¼ steps, up to 600); next to it the unit, beats or seconds.
  3. for all scenes: this setting also replaces the scenes' own transitions.
  4. Own transition of a scene, e.g. 2♩ ◯ = 2 beats circle, ✂ = cut. Crossed out while “for all scenes” is on.
  5. The active scene (white); the pink bar at the bottom shows the progress of the fade.
  6. Pencil: edit the scene (name, own transition, what comes afterwards).

The 15 transitions

11Circlea circle opens from the middle◯

13Pixelsthe new picture appears pixel block by block▦
14Bright areas firstthe bright parts of the new scene come first☀

Length: beats or seconds

  • Beats: the transition starts on the next beat and lasts the chosen number of beats (4 beats = one bar). It stays in time when the tempo changes.
  • Seconds: starts at once and lasts exactly that long, independent of the tempo, good for calm moments.
  • 0 or ✂ is a hard cut: the new scene is there at once.

The Fade fader in the fader bar (and desk channel 4) sets the same length in seven steps: cut, ¼, ½, 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats (or seconds). Type in the bar for any other value.

Which transition applies

The demo and the new project use the bar for most scenes (4 beats crossfade) and give their own transitions only to special moments: a white flash on the pop drop, a circle into the tunnel, glitch for the glitch scene, a cut on the drop.

Edit a scene

  1. Name of the scene.
  2. Transition: like the bar (no own transition), cut, beats or seconds, with the length next to it.
  3. Type of the own transition (all 15).
  4. Afterwards: stays, next after bars, next after seconds, next on a phrase change (LJ-Link).
  5. Overwrite with the current picture, move up and down, delete (asks again).

Afterwards only works when Auto is on (button at the top, fader bar or desk channel 7). With Auto the scenes run as a show on their own; the demo works like this.

Recall

  • Scene buttons in the panel and the fader bar, keys 1–9, arrow keys for previous and next.
  • Desk channel 3 picks the scene, MIDI and OSC (/vjlink/scene/go, /next, /prev) and mapped buttons.
  • Transition type, fade length and “for all scenes” can be mapped too (MAPPING: Transition type, Fade, Fade for all scenes).
  • Scene cues in SHOW and scene blocks on the timeline.

✓Tip

A new recall during a running fade starts the next fade from where the first one was heading, without jumping back.

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
