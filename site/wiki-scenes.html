ShowScenes

The building blocks of a show.

A scene is a snapshot of the layers: which clip runs where, with which colors, effects and opacity. + Save scene stores the current picture; the pencil renames, overwrites, sorts or deletes it.

Transition

SettingChoices
Transitionlike the fader Fade, cut, a number of beats or seconds
Stylefade, or through black
Afterwardsstays, next after bars, next after seconds, next on a phrase change (from LJ-Link)

Afterwards only works when Auto is on (button at the top, fader bar or desk channel 7). With Auto the scenes run as a show on their own; the demo works like this.

Recall

  • Scene buttons in the panel and the fader bar, keys 1–9, arrow keys for previous and next.
  • Desk channel 3 picks the scene, MIDI and OSC (/vjlink/scene/go, /next, /prev) and mapped buttons.
  • Scene cues in SHOW and scene blocks on the timeline.
