Short answer

VJ-Link is a free VJ software for macOS and Windows. It plays 150 live visuals and 50 effects on any number of screens, projectors and LED walls, follows the tempo of the music and can be run completely from a light desk over Art-Net, sACN, MIDI or OSC. It is open source under the Eclipse Public License 2.0.

What the free VJ software can do

  • 150 visuals generated live: fractals, 3D tunnels, particles, retro, op art, audio equalizers and laser looks for a projector in haze.
  • 50 effects in a stack per layer: feedback, kaleidoscope, VHS, bloom, pixel sort, luma key and more, plus live buttons for strobe, flash, blinder and blackout.
  • Up to eight layers with blend modes and five color slots that the light desk sets.
  • Scenes with transitions and an automatic order in time with the music.
  • Projection mapping, several screens as one canvas and a window output for OBS.
  • Shows with a running order, a timeline and timecode in and out.
  • Logos, videos, text clips and live cameras with silhouettes and masks.

Who it is for

VJ-Link is built for lighting jockeys and event technicians who also have to run the screens: instead of a second person at a VJ laptop, the light desk recalls the scenes and the visuals follow the beat by themselves. Beginners start with the demo show; it plays ten scenes on its own.

Questions

Is VJ-Link really free?

Yes. VJ-Link is free and open source under the Eclipse Public License 2.0, without watermark, time limit or account.

Does VJ-Link run on Mac and Windows?

Yes: macOS on Apple chip and Intel, and Windows 10 and 11 (64 bit). A graphics card with WebGL 2 is enough.

Do I need experience as a VJ?

No. Open the demo show on the start screen and it plays by itself; from there you change clips, colors and scenes step by step.

Can I use my own videos and logos?

Yes. MP4, MOV and WebM videos and PNG, JPG and SVG graphics; logos on white or black get their background removed automatically.
