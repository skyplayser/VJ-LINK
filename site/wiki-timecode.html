ShowTimecode

Cues and videos in sync with the desk.

Settings → Timecode. Receive and send can be switched on separately, also both at once. VJ-Link receives Art-Net and MIDI timecode and sends over Art-Net, MIDI timecode and, new in 0.1.17, as LTC over an audio output, with 24, 25, 29.97 DF and 30 fps. As master, the time can come from the SHOW: a timecode cue with the video, like in QLab, or “Timecode from” on any cue.

✓Tip

To only see the timecode of the light desk large at the top, without scenes following it: switch on the clock bar with network timecode.

  1. Receive on/off, below the incoming time and its state.
  2. Source: Art-Net or MIDI timecode (MTC).
  3. Videos: follow the timecode (seek and pause) or are only started.
  4. On dropout: run on for 2 s or stop at once.
  5. Offset: added to the received time.
  6. Send on/off, below the outgoing time.
  7. What to send: VJ-Link is master (own clock, set by the SHOW or the buttons) or pass on the received time.
  8. Frame rate: 24, 25, 29.97 DF, 30.
  9. Send via: Art-Net, MIDI (MTC) and Audio (LTC), one or several at once.
  10. Art-Net target: IP of the desk, or 255.255.255.255 for all.
  11. Output for LTC: the sound card or audio interface.
  12. Channel: on which channel of the output LTC runs (with multi-channel interfaces also 3, 4 …).
  13. Gain of the LTC signal: −18, −12 or −6 dBFS.
  14. Start time of the own clock.
  15. Clock: start, stop, back to the start time.

Receive

A desk or program sets the time. Cues with the start Timecode follow it, videos follow it (seek and pause), with an offset. If the signal drops out, VJ-Link runs on for 2 s or stops at once.

Send: VJ-Link as master

VJ-Link sends its own clock or passes on the received one, for example to a grandMA whose timecode show then fires its cues. As master the clock is set by the SHOW (see the next two sections), by timecode cues (start at a time, stop, locate), by the buttons and by mapping.

Timecode with the video, like in QLab

Start a video and the timecode together, and stop the timecode when the video ends: put a + Timecode cue below the video with the start with previous.

  1. Switch on Send under Settings → Timecode and choose the way (Art-Net, MIDI, LTC).

  2. In SHOW select the video cue and click + Timecode. The new cue lands below it and is already set to with previous.

  3. Under Command keep Start at and enter the time, e.g. 10:00:00:00.

  4. GO: the video and the timecode start together. Pause stops the timecode, at the end of the video it stops at the length of the video. A wait on the timecode cue counts like a pre-wait in QLab.

  1. The video cue, here running with the time left.
  2. The timecode cue below it, with previous: “Timecode with the video from 10:00:00:00”.
  3. + Timecode adds the timecode cue below the selected cue.
  4. GO starts the next cue.
  5. The outgoing timecode at the top, here 3 s into the video.
  1. Start: with previous couples the timecode to the video above.
  2. Command: start at, stop or jump to.
  3. Time at which the timecode starts.
  4. The note says that the timecode runs with the video and stops at its end.

✎Note

A + Timecode cue that is not set to with previous works as before: it starts the clock, which then runs freely until a stop cue.

“Timecode from” on any cue

Shorter: every cue (video, image, camera, black, VJ scene) has Timecode from in its details. While the cue runs, VJ-Link sends this time plus the position in the cue, e.g. 01:00:00:00, after 30 s 01:00:30:00. Pause and end stop the timecode; the next cue with its own time takes over; a cue without a time or Stop all stops it. A VJ scene with a time sets the clock and lets it run freely. The list shows “TC ▲ 01:00:00:00”.

  1. Timecode from: the time at the start of the cue; empty = no timecode.
  2. Off removes the time.
  3. What happens while the cue runs.

LTC over an audio output

LTC (linear timecode, SMPTE 12M) is an audio signal that lighting desks, media servers and recorders read through an audio input. Choose Audio (LTC) under Send via, then the output, the channel and the gain. VJ-Link calculates every frame for the moment it leaves the output, so the timecode matches the picture.

!Warning

Put LTC on its own output or a free channel of an audio interface, not on the channel with the sound of the videos, and not on speakers: LTC sounds like a loud buzz.

✓Tip

Start with −12 dBFS. If the desk shows no or a jumping timecode, try −6 dBFS; if it is distorted, −18 dBFS.

βBeta

Built and tested with software senders and receivers and an own LTC decoder (all frame rates, macOS and Windows), not yet with a real desk.
