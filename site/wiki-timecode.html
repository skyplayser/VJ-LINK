Wiki › Show › Timecode

ShowTimecode

Cues and videos in sync with the desk.

Settings → Timecode. Receive and send can be switched on separately, also both at once, over Art-Net and MIDI timecode, with 24, 25, 29.97 DF and 30 fps.

Receive

A desk or program sets the time. Cues with the start Timecode follow it, videos follow it (seek and pause), with an offset. If the signal drops out, VJ-Link runs on for 2 s or stops at once.

Send

VJ-Link sends its own clock or passes on the received one, for example to a grandMA whose timecode show then fires its cues. The clock is controlled by timecode cues in SHOW (start at a time, stop, locate) and by mapping.

βBeta

Built and tested with software senders and receivers, not yet with a real desk.
