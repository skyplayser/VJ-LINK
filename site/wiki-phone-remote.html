ControlPhone remote

Clips, colours and strobe from the phone.

Control VJ-Link from a phone in the same Wi-Fi: start clips (with preview pictures), change colours and the colour slots, tap the tempo, set strobe speed and intensity, and hold strobe, strobe white and blinder. Nothing to install: the phone opens a page that VJ-Link serves itself, only in the local network.

  1. Phone remote in the list of connections.
  2. Switch: on starts the remote. After every start of VJ-Link it is off.
  3. Network address: the Wi-Fi or LAN of this computer that the phone is in (127.0.0.1 = this computer only).
  4. QR code: scan it with the phone camera; it opens the page and pairs at once.
  5. Or type the address into the phone browser …
  6. … and enter the 8-digit pairing code.

What the phone can do

ControlWhat it does
Clipschoose a layer, tap a clip to start it; Stop layer stops it
Colourschoose slot A–E, tap a colour; Apply to layer gives the selected layer this slot
TAPtap the tempo
Strobe, Strobe White, Blinderactive while the finger is on the button; ends by itself after about one second without signal (e.g. Wi-Fi gone). Safe flashes and the per-device settings still apply

Security

  • Off by default and after every start; on only while the switch is on.
  • Reachable only at the chosen local address, on a free port; no internet, no cloud.
  • Pairing with an 8-digit code, a few attempts per half minute; a new pairing ends the old one.
  • The connection is not encrypted (http). Use it only in your own, trusted network, not in an open guest Wi-Fi.

Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
The phone does not open the pagephone and computer must be in the same network; choose the matching network address; some guest Wi-Fis block devices from each other
macOS: the page loads on the computer, but not on the phoneallow incoming connections for VJ-Link in the macOS firewall
“Session ended. Please pair again.”the remote was switched off or another phone paired; scan the QR code again
No QR codewith 127.0.0.1 there is none (the phone cannot reach it); choose the Wi-Fi or LAN address
