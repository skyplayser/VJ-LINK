LJ-LinkVJ-Link and LJ-Link

The DJ's music drives the visuals.

LJ-Link is the sister project: it reads Pioneer CDJs (Pro DJ Link), Denon Prime (StageLinQ) and Serato and shows the lighting jockey every deck with waveform, cues and BPM. VJ-Link takes from it what the visuals need, so lights, visuals and music run in one beat without anybody tapping.

What VJ-Link gets from LJ-Link

DataWhat VJ-Link does with it
Tempo and beat phase of the master deckthe clock of all visuals, effects, strobes, loops and transitions; the beat lands exactly on the kick
Phrase changesscenes with “next on phrase change” move on when the track changes its part (Auto on)
Track changeshows the Now Playing lower third for 8, 15 or 30 s
Title, artist, coverthe clip Now Playing (empty in LJ-Link's privacy mode)
Waveform and positionthe clip DJ waveform, scrolling like on the player
Bass, mids, highsdrive audio visuals and modulation without a microphone

Back to LJ-Link VJ-Link reports its scene, blackout, master, BPM and tempo source; Open LJ-Link under CONNECT opens LJ-Link's web interface.

Connecting

Nothing to set up: VJ-Link finds LJ-Link on the same computer and in the network by itself (Bonjour) and connects. The LJ-LINK pill at the top turns green, CONNECT shows the decks. Only if LJ-Link runs in another network, enter its address or address:port under CONNECT → LJ-Link.

Scenes on the phrase

  1. Build your scenes (for example Intro, Build, Drop, Breakdown).

  2. Pencil on each scene → Afterwards: next on phrase change.

  3. Switch Auto on. Every time the DJ's track starts a new phrase, VJ-Link moves on to the next scene, with the scene's own transition.

✓Tip

The desk can take over at any time: a scene from the desk or a pad wins, Auto goes on from there.

Club rules with Auto

With Auto on, VJ-Link also follows these fixed rules from the master deck: chorus → scene named „Drop“, up → „Build“, down and outro → „Ambient“, hot cue C → strobe for 4 beats, loop on → effect 1 of layer 2 to 80 %. A rule only fires if a scene with that name exists. With Auto off, the desk alone decides.
