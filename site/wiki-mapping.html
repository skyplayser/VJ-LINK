ControlMapping

Click an element, move a fader, done.

Mapping means: you connect an input from outside (a key, a MIDI fader or pad, a DMX channel from the light desk or an OSC message) to a control in VJ-Link. Like in Daslight, everything you can click can be mapped: faders, buttons, clips, scenes, effects, color slots, the SHOW buttons and more than 500 other controls.

Mapping in four steps

  1. Click MAPPING at the top and choose the kind: Keyboard, MIDI, DMX / Art-Net or OSC (shortcuts ⇧⌘K, ⇧⌘M, ⇧⌘D, ⇧⌘O). Every control that can be mapped now lights up in purple, and a panel opens on the right.

  2. Click the control you want, for example the fader of layer 2. It flashes white: VJ-Link waits for an input.

  3. Press the key, move the MIDI fader or pad, move the channel on the light desk, or send the OSC message. VJ-Link takes the first input that changes clearly.

  4. The control turns green: it is mapped. Map the next one, or end with Done or Esc. Everything is saved with the project.

  2. Every assignable element lights up in purple. Click one, then move the input.

  5. Export and Import the mappings as a file.

How a control reacts

Every control has a type. It decides what a fader, a pad or a key does with it:

Fadermaster, layer opacity, effect 1–3, strobe speed, slot hue, fade timefollows the position 0–100 %pressed = 100 %, released stays
Switchblackout, strobe on, auto, test patternabove half = on, below = offeach press switches on/off

✓Tip

A MIDI button that sends CC instead of notes is recognised as a button: switches change on each press instead of following the value.

Keyboard

Any key without ⌘ or Ctrl can be mapped. Already set when you start: 1–9 scenes, ← → previous and next scene, B blackout, F flash, S strobe, W white flash, T tap, space GO in the SHOW. Keys do nothing while you type in a text field.

MIDI controllers

Controllers are found when you plug them in (no driver needed for class-compliant devices). Faders and knobs send CC, pads and buttons send notes or CC. Pads and buttons get LED feedback: they light up when the clip runs, the scene is active or the switch is on. Controller template loads a ready layout:

ControllerLayoutStatus
Akai APC mini mk2grid = layer × clip, right buttons = scenes, bottom buttons = blackout, strobe, flash, beat, tap, back, next, auto, faders = layers, strobe, effects, transition, masterBuilt

Korg nanoKONTROL2faders = layers 1–4, strobe, effects, master, knobs = effect 1 and slot hues, buttons = scenes, clips, strobe, blackoutBuilt

MIDI clock (tempo) and MIDI timecode come in over the same devices; see tempo and timecode. MIDI can be switched off as a whole under CONNECT.

DMX channels from the light desk

There are two ways to drive VJ-Link from a light desk, and you can use both at once:

WayHowGood for
Fixed channel listpatch VJ-Link as a fixture with 91 channels from a start address; every channel has a fixed meaning (master, scene, layers, colors …)every desk, ready-made fixture profiles, see desk basics
DMX mappingMAPPING → DMX / Art-Net, click a control, move any channel in any universesingle extras outside the list, e.g. a button for the test pattern

While learning, VJ-Link takes the channel that moved the most (at least 20 steps), so a desk that sends all channels all the time is no problem.

OSC

OSC arrives on UDP port 8000 (changeable under CONNECT). Every control has the address /vjlink/ctl/<id> with a value 0–1 (0–127 and 0–255 as whole numbers work too); the ids are listed in the reference. With OSC mapping you can also put any address of your own app on a control: click the control, then send the message.

Change, remove, take along

  • Click a green control in mapping mode: it shows its mappings. Remove one with ×, or map a second input (e.g. a MIDI fader and a desk channel on the same layer).
  • The list in the panel shows all mappings of the chosen kind with target and input.
  • Export saves all mappings (keyboard, MIDI, DMX, OSC) as a file, Import loads them into another project.

iInfo

Mapping and modulation work together: the mapped fader sets the base value, the modulation swings around it.
