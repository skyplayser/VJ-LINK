ControlMapping

Click an element, move a fader, done.

Like in Daslight: everything you can click can be put on a key, a MIDI note or CC, a DMX channel or an OSC address.

  1. MAPPING → Keyboard, MIDI, DMX / Art-Net or OSC (or ⇧⌘K / M / D / O). Every element that can be mapped lights up.

  2. Click an element, then press the key, move the fader or pad, move the DMX channel or send the OSC message.

  3. Green means mapped. Clicking it shows its mappings, to remove one or to map a second input.

  4. Done or Esc ends the mode. The mappings are saved with the project.

MIDI controllers

Controllers are found when you plug them in. Pads and buttons get LED feedback. Controller template loads a ready layout:

ControllerLayoutStatus
Akai APC mini mk2grid = layer × clip, right buttons = scenes, bottom buttons = blackout, strobe, flash, beat, tap, back, next, auto, faders = layers, strobe, effects, transition, masterBuilt
Akai APC mini (mk1)as the mk2Built
Korg nanoKONTROL2faders = layers 1–4, strobe, effects, master, knobs = effect 1 and slot hues, buttons = scenes, clips, strobe, blackoutBuilt

Export and Import save all mappings as a file, for example to take your controller layout to another project.

OSC

OSC arrives on UDP port 8000. Every control has the address /vjlink/ctl/<id> with a value 0–1; the ids are listed in the reference.
