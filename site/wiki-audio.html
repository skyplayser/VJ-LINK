ControlAudio input

Visuals that hear the music.

VJ-Link can listen to the music itself: through a microphone, a line-in or the input of an audio interface. It splits the sound into eight frequency bands, from the deep bass to the highs, and finds the bass hits.

  1. Input: off, or one of the audio inputs of the computer.
  2. Level: 25–400 %. Raise it until the meter moves well, without staying at the right end.
  3. Meter: the loudest band right now; red means too loud.
  4. Works on: only the audio visuals, or all visuals.
  5. Output: where the sound of videos comes out.

Choose an input

  1. Click AUDIO at the top, or Settings → Audio.

  2. Pick the input. macOS asks once for microphone access: allow it (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone).

  3. Set the level so that the meter moves well with the music.

  4. Choose Works on. The AUDIO button at the top shows the input and eight small level bars.

Works onWhat happens
Audio visualsonly visuals that are made for audio (equalizers, oscilloscope, spectrum circle …) follow the input; everything else keeps running in time
All visualsevery visual reacts to bass, mids and highs

Clips in the grid that are driven by the input right now get a small mark. Per layer you can also set the drive to Audio, then the layer pulses with the bass instead of the beat, see layers. Audio modulations use the same bands.

Which input is best

InputRatingNote
Line-in from the DJ mixer (booth or rec out)Recommendedclean signal, independent of the room
Audio interface inputRecommendedlike line-in, with a level knob
Built-in microphoneWorksworks, but reacts to talking and the room
No input, LJ-Link onlyWorksbands come from the waveform of the master deck

Audio output

On the same settings page, Output picks where the sound of videos comes out (cues and video clips with sound switched on).
