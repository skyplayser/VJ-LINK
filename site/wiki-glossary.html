HelpGlossary

The words used in VJ-Link.

TermMeaning
Art-NetDMX over the network (UDP). Universes count from 0.
sACNStreaming ACN (E1.31), DMX over the network as multicast. Universes count from 1.
Universe512 DMX channels.
ClipAnything a layer can play: a visual, a graphic, a video, a text, a camera.
LayerOne level of the picture with its own clip, effects, colors and opacity.
Color slotOne of five show colors A–E that the desk can set.
DriveWhere a layer's pulsing comes from: audio, beat or none.
SceneA saved state of all layers, recalled with a transition.
CueOne step in the SHOW running order.
PhraseA part of a track, usually 16 or 32 bars; LJ-Link reports when a new one starts.
CanvasA surface on the projector picture in projection mapping, or the shared picture across several screens.
Corner pinFitting a rectangle to four corners on a wall or box.
LFOLow frequency oscillator: a slow wave that moves a fader in time.
TimecodeA running time (hours:minutes:seconds:frames) that keeps shows in sync.
Lightjockey (LJ)The person who runs light (and here video) live to the DJ's music.
