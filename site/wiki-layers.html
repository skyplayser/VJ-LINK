VisualsLayers and clips

How the picture is built from up to eight layers.

Layers

A project has one to eight layers. The bottom layer is the base, the ones above lie on top of it. Each layer plays one clip at a time from its row in the clip grid.

  • + Clips at the end of a row opens the library: tap a visual, a camera, a video or a graphic to add it to the row, tap again to remove it.
  • Click a clip to start it, click it again to stop it. The running clip has a green frame.
  • + Layer adds a layer, the × next to its name removes it. Layer names can be changed in the layer panel.

Per layer

SettingWhat it does
Opacitythe fader next to the name and in the fader bar
Blend modeNormal, Add, Screen or Multiply. Add and Screen make dark parts transparent.
Color sourceOriginal, one color slot, several slots, a gradient or a rainbow
DriveAuto: audio when the audio input works on this layer, otherwise the beat. Beat: only the BPM. Audio: only the audio. Static: no pulsing.
PositionX, Y, size and rotation of every visual; the visual still fills the whole screen
Effectsa stack of up to six effects, see effects

Color slots

Five color slots A–E sit on the right. They are the colors of the show: the desk sets them over DMX (dimmer and RGB per slot), MIDI or OSC, and every layer that uses a slot changes with them. With several slots, laser looks give each beam its own color and move them on every eight beats.

Laser layers

The laser looks are made for a projector in haze: a point in the picture becomes a beam, a line a sheet of light, a circle a cone. They lie in the upper third by themselves. Strength makes lines thicker so they carry more light. With XY override their own movement stops and X/Y set the position directly, for example from moving-head channels of the desk.

αAlpha: untested

The laser looks have not been tried with a projector in real haze yet.
