Light desksAll desks

Every common desk and how it reaches VJ-Link.

Any desk that can send Art-Net or sACN can run VJ-Link: VJ-Link behaves like a fixture with 91 channels. Desks and programs with MIDI or OSC can also reach every control through mapping. The other way round VJ-Link sends timecode (Art-Net and MIDI) to desks that run timecode shows.
