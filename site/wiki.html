What VJ-Link does

VJ-Link plays live visuals on screens, projectors and LED walls. The light desk leads: it recalls scenes, sets the colors, the brightness, the strobe and the blackout. The tempo comes from the music, best from LJ-Link, so everything moves in time without anybody tapping.

  • Local: no account, no cloud, no telemetry. Only the update check and a problem report you send yourself use the internet.
  • Free and open source for macOS and Windows, under the Eclipse Public License 2.0 like LJ-Link: source code.
  • English and German, chosen from the system language and switchable on the start screen.
