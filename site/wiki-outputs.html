OutputsScreens and canvas

Projectors, LED walls and capture.

Every connected screen, projector or LED processor can be an output. OUTPUT lists them with their resolution; Open as output puts a full-screen window on it. All outputs share one clock, so they stay in sync.

  1. Screens: every connected screen, projector and LED wall.
  2. Open as output / Close: one click per screen.
  3. Arrangement: same picture on all, canvas, separate (own image per output) or projection mapping.
  4. Canvas: drag the screens to where they hang; the edges snap.
  5. Side by side / Stacked arrange them at once; a click on a screen shows exact values.

Arrangement

ModeWhat happens
Same picture on allevery output shows the whole picture in its own resolution
Canvasarrange the screens freely (drag, snap, type values, side by side or stacked); each output shows its part, visuals and videos run seamlessly across all

Projection mappingcanvases with corner pin, grid warp and masks on the picture of each projector, see projection mapping

Each output its own image

With Separate every output is on its own: it shows one of up to five images in full, in its own resolution. Each image has its own layers, clip grid, effects and brightness. So two identical screens can show two different things, without projection mapping. NDI® outputs choose their image the same way.

  1. Arrangement Separate: own image per output.
  2. Every open output with the image it shows.
  3. Image per output: choose image 1 to 5 right in the box of the output.
  1. Image tiles: live preview of every image; a click edits it (clips, layers, effects), double-click renames it, the slider sets its brightness.
  2. + Image adds one more (copy of image 1, up to five).
  3. The clip grid shows the layers of the image being edited.
  4. Outputs: which image each output shows, the same choice as in OUTPUT.

  1. Open the screens (and NDI outputs) as outputs.

  2. Choose Separate. Every further output gets a image of its own right away; missing images are created as a copy of image 1.

  3. In VISUALS click a image tile and fill its clip grid; the other images keep running.

  4. Change which output shows which image at any time, in OUTPUT or next to the clip grid.

  • Image area, device, strobe and blinder per output and output profiles work as with every arrangement.
  • Scenes save and recall all images. The SHOW timeline runs on one image of your choice; the other images can keep their own scenes.
  • Desk channels 71–75 set the brightness of the images, 76–91 the opacity of their layers, as in projection mapping. MIDI and OSC can do the same.
  • A fixed resolution applies to every output: each renders its image in that size.

✓Tip

Every image costs computing power. On a slower computer use two images rather than five, or a fixed resolution such as 1280 × 720.

Output resolution

Next to the preview in VISUALS, Resolution decides in which size the visuals are rendered. Auto fits almost always; a fixed size or aspect ratio helps with LED processors, recordings, portrait screens and slower computers.

  1. Resolution button: shows Auto, the pixel size or the aspect ratio.
  2. Auto, Pixels or Aspect.
  3. Aspect ratios: 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, 5:4, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 21:9, 32:9, 3:1, 4:1, 6:1.
  4. Your own ratio, e.g. 5:2 or 2.35:1.
  5. On outputs with a different aspect ratio: fit, fill or stretch.
  6. The preview takes the new shape at once.
ModeWhat happensExample
Autoevery output renders in its own full sizeprojector and LED wall with different sizes
Pixelsrendered in exactly width × height (16 to 8192), then fitted onto every output; presets from 1280 × 720 to 3840 × 2160, portrait and LED strips, ⇄ swaps portrait and landscapean LED processor that wants 1920 × 1080, or a slow laptop at 1280 × 720
Aspectfixed aspect ratio, as large as fits each output9:16 for portrait steles, 32:9 for a wide stage
  • Fit shows the whole picture; where the shape differs, black bars remain.
  • Fill fills the output; what sticks out is cropped equally on both sides.
  • Stretch pulls the picture onto the output and distorts it.

✎Note

With Canvas the arrangement of the screens sets the size, so this setting does not apply there. With projection mapping the picture is rendered in this size and then spread onto the canvases. The setting is saved with the project.

Window output

Instead of full screen, an output can be a normal window with a fixed picture size (720p up to 4K, also portrait and 1280 × 1024). Capture it in OBS or with Syphon tools. It also works as a projector in canvas and projection mapping.

✎Note

NDI® is built in: see NDI output. Native Syphon and Spout are planned.

Only part of a screen in use, for example by an LED controller? See image area, devices and profiles.

Test patterns

Nine test patterns to step through: color bars, grid, grid across all, numbers, grey scale, white, red, green and blue, each with the resolution, name and position of every output. Also on a key or desk channel: test pattern, next, previous.

✓Tip

With only one screen an output covers the interface. Esc or Close in the main window ends it.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
