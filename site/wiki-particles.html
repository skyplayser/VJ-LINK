VisualsParticle objects and 3D models

3D bodies of glowing dust that fly apart on the beat, your own OBJ and STL models included.

A particle object is a 3D body made of thousands of fine, glowing dust grains that sit on its surface. On the beat, the bar or the bass they fly apart and come back to the shape. The grains add up their light like dust in a spotlight: where many lie on top of each other, e.g. at the rim of the sphere, it glows brighter. VJ-Link brings five bodies (cross, cube, sphere, torus, star), and you can load your own 3D models as OBJ or STL, e.g. a logo.

!Strong graphics card

Particle objects need a strong graphics card. Target: three screens at 60 frames per second on an Apple M2 (or a comparable graphics card) and up, with up to 40 thousand particles. On weaker computers lower Count.

Start a particle object

  1. Open GALLERY → 3D and space or + Clips in a layer row and choose Particle cross, cube, sphere, torus or star.

  2. Click the clip in the row. It explodes on bar 1 and rotates slowly.

  3. Click the layer name: on the right the panel shows the section Particle object with all settings.

  4. On an overlay layer the dust glows over the visuals below; Add makes it brighter, Normal keeps the visuals below darker.

  1. A built-in body in the clip row (here the particle cross, playing). Own 3D models carry the badge 3D, see below.
  2. The preview: the cross as dust in slot B over the rings of layer 1.
  3. The section Particle object in the layer panel.

Settings

  1. Effect: Particles, Drift, Melt, Jelly, Dissolve, Twist, Shatter or None, see effects.
  2. A short description of the chosen effect.
  3. Trigger: every beat, bar 1, every 2 or 4 bars, Bass (audio) or Hold (fader).
  4. The note for the chosen trigger (here: bass).
  5. Rotation on / off: switches the three rotation speeds.
  6. Solid shape on / off: at rest a closed surface instead of dust, see below. On by default for own models and all body effects, off for Particles on the built-in bodies.
  7. Explosion (with the other effects Strength): how far the particles fly or how strong the effect is (0 = the shape stays as it is).
  8. Return: how long they take to come back, in beats.
  9. Scatter (only with Particles): 0 % flies straight out from the surface, 100 % in all directions.
  10. Rotation per axis: speed around X (tilt), Y (turn) and Z (roll) in turns per minute, negative = the other way.
  1. Angle X / Y / Z: the fixed position of the body (−180° to 180°); the rotation turns on from there.
  2. Angle Z (roll); the layer's Rotation under Position rolls the picture in addition.
  3. Particle size: size of the dust grains (they vary by themselves; a few are larger and softer).
  4. Count: 5 to 200 thousand particles, default 40 thousand. More grains make the dust finer, not brighter.
  5. The hint about the graphics card. Reset particles above it restores all defaults.

A double click on a slider sets it to 0. Return, size and count have no 0; a double click sets them to their default. Position, size and rotation of the layer (section Position) act on particle objects as on every visual.

Effects: how the body behaves

The effect decides what the object does on the trigger. Particles bursts it open into dust, Drift lets it blow away as dust behind a glowing edge; all other effects keep the body together and change the body itself: it melts, wobbles, burns away, twists or breaks into solid pieces, with a matching surface (wet and glossy when melting, glossy as jelly, glowing at the edge when it burns away). Every effect uses the same trigger, strength and return.

EffectWhat the body does on the trigger
Particlesbursts open on the trigger: the grains shoot out with a short flash, swirl in a current and come back to their place
Drifta glowing edge sweeps through the body from top left to bottom right; behind it the body crumbles into dust that drifts away, getting smaller and paler; then it reassembles in reverse
Meltgives way from the top, slumps into a thick mass, drips in places and runs out into a glossy puddle (seen slightly from above), then grows back up
Jellya glossy body like jelly: squashes from the bottom, stretches and swings on with ripples over the surface; with Bass or Hold it wobbles all the time; a light wobble on the beats in between
Dissolvean edge moves from the top down through the body and glows; above it the body rises as smoke, below it stays solid; on the way back the smoke settles into the body again
Twistthe body twists like a wrung-out cloth, top against bottom, and turns back
Shatterthe separate parts of the model (e.g. each letter of a logo, a butterfly next to it) fly apart one by one, spin around their own centre and join again; a body made of one piece breaks into solid pieces instead
Nonejust the object, without an effect; trigger, strength and return are hidden; rotation, angles and position still work
  • Melt, Dissolve and Twist rise more slowly than Particles and Shatter (about one beat), Drift takes about one and a half beats; rise and return always fit before the next trigger. With Every beat the slow effects do not come back completely in between; bar 1 or longer suits them better.
  • Gravity when melting and the smoke when dissolving always point down and up on the screen, whatever the rotation of the object.
  • All body effects have Solid shape on by default, also for the built-in bodies; switched off, the body is made of dust and still melts, wobbles, twists or breaks.
  • Use None to put a logo or object into the show without any effect.

Solid shape: logos stay logos

With Solid shape on, VJ-Link draws the model itself: its triangles with smooth curves and sharp edges, its colors and textures, lit like a studio product shot (soft key light, fill and back light, reflections). A logo looks like the logo. Only the effect breaks it up: with Particles the body opens up where the dust flies out, and on the way back it closes again. The small pulse on the beats between two bursts does not break it up.

  • Own 3D models (OBJ, STL) and all body effects have Solid shape on by default; Particles on the built-in bodies off. Each layer remembers its choice.
  • Coarse models are refined when they are loaded (no edge longer than about 5 % of the object), so that melting, wobbling and twisting stay soft; very large models are simplified to at most 400 thousand triangles.
  • Models loaded before this version have no body yet and still show the surface made of grains: load them once more from their file.
  • On a light desk: layer.2.particles.solid switches it, e.g. a logo that only turns into dust on the drop.

Triggers

TriggerWhen the particles fly apart
Every beaton every beat; return is at most just under one beat
Bar 1on the first beat of every bar; a small pulse on the other beats
Every 2 / 4 barsevery 8 or 16 beats, for build-ups and drops
Bass (audio)the harder the bass, the further they fly; quiet bass keeps the shape closed. Source: audio input or LJ-Link. Explosion is the strength, Return how fast they gather again.
Hold (fader)no beat: the Explosion fader holds the particles apart directly, e.g. from a fader on the light desk

The beat triggers run on VJ-Link's beat clock (LJ-Link, Ableton Link, MIDI clock or tap), so every output explodes at the same moment.

Own 3D models (OBJ, STL)

  1. Drag an .obj or .stl file onto VJ-Link, or use + Clips → + Load file. Keep the .mtl file and the textures of an OBJ in the same folder.

  2. VJ-Link converts the model once into a point cloud (usually well under a second) and puts it into the layer as a clip with the badge 3D.

  3. Set explosion, rotation and particles as for the built-in bodies.

  1. Your 3D models: every loaded model of this project.
  2. Tap a model to put it into the row of this layer, tap again to take it out (here a self-made demo diamond).
  3. × deletes the model from the library, from all layers, scenes and cues (asks first).
  4. + Load file loads more models, videos or graphics.

  • The model is centered and scaled to the same size as the built-in bodies; size and position come from the layer (section Position).
  • Models are kept in the library of the computer and saved with the project. Save as and the show package copy them into the media folder next to the project file, so the project moves to another computer.
  • Logos and lettering: with Solid shape they stay readable at rest; more Count and a smaller particle size make fine letters sharper as dust.

Change of shape

If a layer switches from one particle object to another (click, scene, chase), the particles fly from the old shape into the new one within about one and a half beats. This works between all built-in bodies and own models.

Light desk, MIDI and OSC

Every setting is a control of the layer and can be mapped (MAPPING) and modulated. The OSC addresses follow the control IDs:

Control ID (layer 2)Range
layer.2.particles.mode8 steps: particles, melt, jelly, dissolve, twist, shatter, none, drift
layer.2.particles.explode0–100 % (explosion or strength)
layer.2.particles.trigger6 steps: beat, bar 1, 2 bars, 4 bars, bass, hold
layer.2.particles.ret · .scatter¼–4 beats · 0–100 %
layer.2.particles.spin · .solidrotation on / off · solid shape on / off
layer.2.particles.rpmX · .rpm · .rpmZ−30 to 30 turns per minute (X, Y, Z)
layer.2.particles.angX · .angY · .angZ−180° to 180°
layer.2.particles.size · .count30–300 % · 5–200 thousand

Performance

  • All grains are drawn by the graphics card in one pass (with Solid shape in two: first the surface, then the dust). Every grain is a small square with soft light, not a real sphere.
  • The light of the grains adds up in a picture with high precision; bright areas roll off softly instead of clipping to white. Grains smaller than a pixel are not enlarged but drawn fainter, so they do not flicker while rotating.
  • Every output window draws the picture itself. Three screens therefore cost about three times as much as one.
  • Not yet measured on three real screens; if it stutters, lower Count first.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCause and fix
Stutters on several outputsgraphics card too weak: lower Count, fewer effects on this layer
Bass trigger does nothingno audio: choose an audio input under Settings → Audio or connect LJ-Link; raise Explosion
Model stays greySTL has no colors, or the .mtl and textures of the OBJ were not in the same folder; use a slot as color source or load the OBJ again from its folder
Model looks inside out or darkits faces point inward: export it from the 3D program with outward normals
Lettering is hard to readSolid shape on, more Count; rotation off and the angles to 0
Logo looks grainy instead of solidSolid shape on; if it was loaded with an older version, load the model again from its file
The cloud covers everythingExplosion lower or Scatter lower; on the bass trigger also less input level
