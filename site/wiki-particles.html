VisualsParticle objects and 3D models

3D bodies of sticks and dots that fly apart on the beat, your own OBJ and STL models included.

A particle object is a 3D body made of thousands of short sticks or dots that sit on its surface. On the beat, the bar or the bass they fly apart and come back to the shape. VJ-Link brings five bodies (cross, cube, sphere, torus, star), and you can load your own 3D models as OBJ or STL.

!Strong graphics card

Particle objects need a strong graphics card. Target: three screens at 60 frames per second on an Apple M2 (or a comparable graphics card) and up, with up to 40 thousand particles. On weaker computers lower Count, or set Length to 0 (dots).

Start a particle object

  1. Open GALLERY → 3D and space or + Clips in a layer row and choose Particle cross, cube, sphere, torus or star.

  2. Click the clip in the row. It explodes on bar 1 and rotates slowly.

  3. Click the layer name: on the right the panel shows the section Particle object with all settings.

  4. On an overlay layer use the blend mode Normal; with Add the particles look pale over bright visuals.

  1. A built-in body in the clip row (here the particle cross, playing).
  2. An own 3D model carries the badge 3D.
  3. The preview: the cross explodes over the rings of layer 1.
  4. The section Particle object in the layer panel.

Settings

  1. Trigger: every beat, bar 1, every 2 or 4 bars, Bass (audio) or Hold (fader).
  2. The note for the chosen trigger (here: bass).
  3. Rotation on / off: switches the three rotation speeds.
  4. Explosion: how far the particles fly (0 = the shape stays closed).
  5. Return: how long they take to come back, in beats.
  6. Scatter: 0 % flies straight out from the surface, 100 % in all directions.
  7. Rotation per axis: speed around X (tilt), Y (turn) and Z (roll) in turns per minute, negative = the other way.
  1. Angle X / Y / Z: the fixed position of the body (−180° to 180°); the rotation turns on from there.
  2. Angle Z (roll); the layer's Rotation under Position rolls the picture in addition.
  3. Particle size: thickness of sticks and dots.
  4. Length: 100 % = sticks, 0 = round dots. Dots show fine logos and lettering better.
  5. Count: 5 to 200 thousand particles, default 40 thousand.
  6. The hint about the graphics card. Reset particles above it restores all defaults.

A double click on a slider sets it to 0. Return, size and count have no 0; a double click sets them to their default. Position, size and rotation of the layer (section Position) act on particle objects as on every visual.

Triggers

TriggerWhen the particles fly apart
Every beaton every beat; return is at most just under one beat
Bar 1on the first beat of every bar; a small pulse on the other beats
Every 2 / 4 barsevery 8 or 16 beats, for build-ups and drops
Bass (audio)the harder the bass, the further they fly; quiet bass keeps the shape closed. Source: audio input or LJ-Link. Explosion is the strength, Return how fast they gather again.
Hold (fader)no beat: the Explosion fader holds the particles apart directly, e.g. from a fader on the light desk

The beat triggers run on VJ-Link's beat clock (LJ-Link, Ableton Link, MIDI clock or tap), so every output explodes at the same moment.

Own 3D models (OBJ, STL)

  1. Drag an .obj or .stl file onto VJ-Link, or use + Clips → + Load file. Keep the .mtl file and the textures of an OBJ in the same folder.

  2. VJ-Link converts the model once into a point cloud (usually well under a second) and puts it into the layer as a clip with the badge 3D.

  3. Set explosion, rotation and particles as for the built-in bodies.

  1. Your 3D models: every loaded model of this project.
  2. Tap a model to put it into the row of this layer, tap again to take it out (here a self-made demo diamond).
  3. × deletes the model from the library, from all layers, scenes and cues (asks first).
  4. + Load file loads more models, videos or graphics.

  • The model is centered and scaled to the same size as the built-in bodies; size and position come from the layer (section Position).
  • Models are kept in the library of the computer and saved with the project. Save as and the show package copy them into the media folder next to the project file, so the project moves to another computer.
  • Logos and lettering: Length 0 (dots) and a smaller particle size keep fine letters readable.

Change of shape

If a layer switches from one particle object to another (click, scene, chase), the particles fly from the old shape into the new one within about one and a half beats. This works between all built-in bodies and own models.

Light desk, MIDI and OSC

Every setting is a control of the layer and can be mapped (MAPPING) and modulated. The OSC addresses follow the control IDs:

Control ID (layer 2)Range
layer.2.particles.explode0–100 %
layer.2.particles.trigger6 steps: beat, bar 1, 2 bars, 4 bars, bass, hold
layer.2.particles.ret · .scatter¼–4 beats · 0–100 %
layer.2.particles.spinrotation on / off
layer.2.particles.rpmX · .rpm · .rpmZ−30 to 30 turns per minute (X, Y, Z)
layer.2.particles.angX · .angY · .angZ−180° to 180°
layer.2.particles.size · .length · .count30–300 % · 0–300 % · 5–200 thousand

Performance

  • All particles are drawn by the graphics card in one pass; the sticks are flat pictures of a capsule (two triangles each), not real cylinders.
  • Every output window draws the picture itself. Three screens therefore cost about three times as much as one.
  • Measured on an Apple M4 Max: 40 thousand sticks add about 0.5 ms per 1080p window, 200 thousand about 1.3 ms.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCause and fix
Stutters on several outputsgraphics card too weak: lower Count, set Length to 0, fewer effects on this layer
Bass trigger does nothingno audio: choose an audio input under Settings → Audio or connect LJ-Link; raise Explosion
Model stays greySTL has no colors, or the .mtl and textures of the OBJ were not in the same folder; use a slot as color source or load the OBJ again from its folder
Model looks inside out or darkits faces point inward: export it from the 3D program with outward normals
Lettering is hard to readLength 0 (dots), smaller Particle size, more Count; rotation off and the angles to 0
The cloud covers everythingExplosion lower or Scatter lower; on the bass trigger also less input level
