VisualsCameras and person detection

Live camera looks for the dance floor and the stage.

Any video input works: a webcam, a capture card (for a camera on HDMI or SDI) or the OBS Virtual Camera. Library → Search cameras asks once for the camera permission and lists all inputs; a camera then becomes a clip like any other, also as a cue in SHOW.

Looks

LookWhat you see
Normalthe camera picture
Silhouettepeople as a filled shape, black or in the layer color
Outlinea glowing line around the person
Dot matrix, ASCII, halftonedots, characters or print dots by brightness, cell size adjustable
Thermal, duotonefalse colors, or slot A for dark and slot B for bright
Mask positiveon the top layer: the visuals below are only visible inside the person
Mask negativeon the top layer: the person is cut out of the visuals

Person detection

Silhouette, outline and the masks need to know where the person is. VJ-Link uses MediaPipe person segmentation for this: it runs on the computer itself, without internet and without a green screen. The fader Tightness sets how close the shape follows the person.

βBeta

Tested with one person in front of a webcam. Several people, people far away on a big stage and stage lighting with haze and backlight are not tested yet.

✓Tip

The masks work on all layers below them, so put the camera layer at the very top.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
