ControlTempo and beat

One clock for everything, fed by the music.

All visuals, effects, strobes, LFOs, loops and scene changes run on one clock. Its tempo comes from the best source available:

  1. BPM: click and type a tempo (e.g. 126) and press Enter.
  2. Source: AUTO and the source in use, or FIXED when you picked one.
  3. Source menu: Auto, or fix LJ-Link, Ableton Link, OSC, MIDI clock or manual.
  4. TAP: tap in time (or key T); a few taps set the tempo.
  5. Beat lights: the four beats of the bar.

Next to the BPM, AUTO picks the source by itself (LJ-Link before Ableton Link before OSC before MIDI clock before tap). Click it to fix one source. If a source drops out, the clock keeps running at the last tempo without a jump.

✎Note

Ableton Link needs Carabiner (Deep Symmetry) as its own program. The Link SDK is GPL, so VJ-Link does not contain it. See CONNECT → Ableton Link.

Tempo × and half time

Tempo × (MIDI, OSC or desk channel 5) multiplies the tempo for the visuals only: ½ for heavy half-time drops, 2 for double time. The BPM display stays at the real tempo.
