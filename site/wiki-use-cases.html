StartUse cases

Typical setups and how VJ-Link fits in.

Club with LED wall and grandMA

LJ-Link reads the CDJs, VJ-Link gets the tempo and the phrase changes from it. The grandMA has VJ-Link patched as a fixture over Art-Net: one executor per scene, the color slots follow the light. The LED wall is the output, in its native resolution.

Mobile DJ with one projector

One laptop, one projector, no desk. Open the demo or a project, add the logo of the couple or the company as a clip, switch on Auto and let the scenes run in time. A MIDI controller like the APC mini gives you clips and scenes on pads.

Event with several screens

Choose Canvas under OUTPUT and arrange the screens: one picture runs seamlessly across all of them. Videos and sponsor logos as cues in SHOW, the headliner show started by timecode from the desk.

Projection onto a set

Projection mapping: lay canvases onto boxes, columns and walls, bend them with a grid, black out doors with masks. Each canvas can show its own picture with its own layers.

Dance floor camera

A webcam on the DJ booth, the look Mask positive on the top layer: the visuals only appear inside the people. Person detection runs on the computer itself.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
